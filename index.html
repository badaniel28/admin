Purpose

Vision, Mission & Goals

Leading Estancia with a vision for inclusive growth, a mission for trusted population service, and goals that empower communities.

Vision

Our Vision

By 2025, the Provincial Population Office will be the trusted population information partner across Region VI—dynamic, people-centered, tech-enabled, and deeply rooted in local culture.

We envision a future where Estancia's people thrive through smarter planning, stronger partnerships, and responsive services.

Mission

Our Mission

We equip local government units, partner agencies, and communities with reliable population insights, practical guidance, and inclusive programs that improve quality of life across Iloilo.

Our work is centered on evidence, collaboration, and service delivery that helps people and leaders make better decisions.

Goals

Our Goals

  1. Build a responsive, up-to-date population data system for smarter planning.
  2. Deliver timely information that strengthens local programs and stakeholder decisions.
  3. Create learning opportunities that empower citizens through population and development awareness.
  4. Adopt modern tools and efficient processes for better data management and service delivery.
Values

Core Values

  • Service: Put people first, always.
  • Innovation: Seek new ways to improve population programs.
  • Excellence: Deliver high-quality, dependable work.
  • Integrity: Act honestly and transparently in every collaboration.
  • Teamwork: Achieve more together with respect and unity.
